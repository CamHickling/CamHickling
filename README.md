#### 🥋 A Taekwondo flow study that couldn't have run without its tools

The UBC Brain, Attention and Reality Lab needed cameras, heart rate and spoken narration recorded together
and lined up precisely. Nothing available could do it, so I built the pipeline from capture to analysis.

- **[Iris](https://github.com/CamHickling/Iris)** runs each lab session: every camera, the heart-rate strap and
  the microphone from one app, on one shared timeline.
- **[FlowAnalytics](https://github.com/CamHickling/FlowAnalytics)** turns each session into a synced review
  video and data ready to code.

**Impact:** 10 researchers · 50 participants · 60+ trials · **no lost sessions** · **90+ hours** of manual
alignment cut · made motion and facial analysis possible. The study is now in analysis, ahead of publication.

#### 👁️ [SocialEyes](https://github.com/CamHickling/SocialEyes): realistic social media experiments with eye tracking

No existing tool offered a realistic social media feed that researchers control, with eye tracking built in.
SocialEyes runs an Instagram-style feed on a phone while participants wear eye-tracking glasses, and shows
exactly where people looked on each post.

- **Users:** a team of 10 UBC researchers studying attention and gaze on social media. **Pilot underway.**
- **Shipped:** a phone app, a desktop app with a guided study builder and a Windows installer, and the
  analysis from raw gaze to tidy tables
- **Product calls:** never delete data automatically; keep analysis blinded until the plan is fixed; ship
  unproven features marked *Experimental*
